🧠 Methodology & Interpretability (Grad-CAM)

Two-Stage Fine-Tuning

Phase 1 freezes the pre-trained ImageNet backbone to train the custom dense classification head (Adam, $\eta=10^{-3}$). Phase 2 unfreezes the top 30 convolutional layers for discriminative fine-tuning at a low learning rate (Adam, $\eta=10^{-5}$), preserving lower-level edge/texture detectors while tailoring high-level canine representations.

Grad-CAM Feature Saliency

Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping computes the gradient of the predicted breed score with respect to the feature activation maps of layer top_conv. This highlights the exact anatomical regions (ears, snout shape, coat pattern) driving the model's prediction.
